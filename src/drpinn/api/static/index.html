9 · How to read this limits of every output

  • Triage: ranks photos for research; the threshold was set for ≥ 90 % sensitivity on APTOS validation photos and may not carry over to other cameras.
  • Quality flags: compare the photo with the training photos; they are not a validated gradability check.
  • Grad-CAM: a coarse localisation aid, not a lesion detector; it can look convincing for wrong predictions.
  • Shear map: a ranking under assumed boundary conditions. Whether high-shear regions relate to lesions is the open research question tested in M7; it is not a prediction of future lesions.
  • Optic disc: placing it by click is more reliable than the estimate.
